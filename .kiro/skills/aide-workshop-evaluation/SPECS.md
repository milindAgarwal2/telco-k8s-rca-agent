# Workshop evaluation skill specification

## Intent

Compare AIDE-assisted and baseline outcomes under controlled, reproducible
conditions using product and process measures.

## Contract

Task, revision, model, tools, environment, and validation remain fixed across
conditions. Setup cost is separate from subsequent-task performance, and raw
measures remain visible.

## Failure handling

Protocol deviations, missing telemetry, learning effects, and incomplete runs
are recorded explicitly and included in limitations.
