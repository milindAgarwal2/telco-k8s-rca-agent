# Change review skill specification

## Intent

Assess a proposed change against approved intent, contracts, repository policy,
and verification evidence.

## Contract

Actionable findings are prioritized, source-linked, impact-focused, and
separate from questions. The review covers unintended scope, compatibility,
security, operations, and test adequacy without approving or merging.

## Failure handling

An incomplete diff, missing base, or absent requirements is disclosed as a
review limitation rather than silently assumed.
