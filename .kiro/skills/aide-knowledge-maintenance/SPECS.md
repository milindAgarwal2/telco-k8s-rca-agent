# Knowledge maintenance skill specification

## Intent

Keep reviewed knowledge synchronized with verified repository changes while
preserving evidence, review status, and graph-ingestion readiness.

## Contract

Updates are scoped, revision-aware, source-linked, and distinguish facts,
inference, observations, proposed state, and unknowns. Generated pages remain
draft until subject-matter expert review.

## Failure handling

Contradictory or insufficient evidence is recorded for review. Generated claim
stores and external knowledge systems are not modified implicitly.
