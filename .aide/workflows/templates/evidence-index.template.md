## Evidence handling notes

- Do not include credentials, personal data, production records, or large
  copied source blocks.
- Prefer stable source paths, revisions, test names, command summaries, and
  redacted observation references.
- Preserve complete failure output in its approved local location and link to
  it rather than hiding or rewriting the failure.
